There are three condemned prisoners A, B and C. The governor has announced that one of the three, chosen at random, has been pardoned, but doesn't say which.
Please tell me the name of one of the other prisoners B or C who will be executed. I already know that at least one of them will be executed so you will not be divulging any information.
The warden thinks for a minute and then asks how he should choose between B or C in case both are to be executed.
"In that case," A tells him, "simply flip a coin (when I'm not around) to choose randomly between the two."
Reluctantly, the warden agrees and the next day tells A that B will be executed.
