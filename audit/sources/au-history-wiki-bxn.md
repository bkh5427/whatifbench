"Jean Richer and Giovanni Domenico Cassini measured the parallax of Mars between Paris and Cayenne in French Guiana when Mars was at its closest to Earth in 1672."
"They arrived at a figure for the solar parallax of 9.5″, equivalent to an Earth–Sun distance of about 22000 Earth radii."
"The various results were collated by Jérôme Lalande to give a figure for the solar parallax of 8.6″."
"Direct radar measurements of the distances to Venus and Mars became available in the early 1960s."
"In 2012, the IAU simply used the 2009 estimate to redefine the astronomical unit as a conventional unit of length directly tied to the metre (exactly 149597870700 m)."
