Author: Kabir Srivastav
In 2002, Britney Gallivan derived a formula estimating the length of paper lost when it is folded repeatedly in half along the same side.
In this paper, we generalize and refine Gallivan's model.
We first derive the corresponding lost-length formulas for alternate-side folding and apply them to both square and rectangular paper.
