"A planimeter, also known as a platometer, is a measuring instrument used to determine the area of an arbitrary two-dimensional shape."
"The operator sets the wheel, turns the counter to zero, and then traces the pointer around the perimeter of the shape. When the tracing is complete, the scales at the measuring wheel show the shape's area."
