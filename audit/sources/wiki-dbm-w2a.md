"dBm or dBmW (decibel-milliwatts) is a unit of power level expressed using a logarithmic decibel (dB) scale respective to one milliwatt (mW)."
"A power level of 0 dBm corresponds to a power of 1 milliwatt. An increase in level of 10 dB is equivalent to a ten-fold increase in power."
