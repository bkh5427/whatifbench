NASA's Mars Fleet Will Still Conduct Science While Lying Low
Nov. 10, 2023
NASA will hold off sending commands to its Mars fleet for two weeks, from Nov. 11 to 25, while Earth and the Red Planet are on opposite sides of the Sun.
Called Mars solar conjunction, this phenomenon happens every two years.
