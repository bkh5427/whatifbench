By 1950, repeated measurements by Essen established a result of 299792.5±3.0 km/s.
Historically the speed of light was used together with timing measurements to determine a value for the astronomical unit (AU).
Radar systems measure the distance to a target by the time it takes a radio-wave pulse to return to the radar antenna after being reflected by the target.
