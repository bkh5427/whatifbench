±1.5 mm for dimensions up to 150 mm, ±2.0 mm for dimensions in the range 150 to 600 mm, and ±3.0 mm for dimensions above 600 mm.
The most used of this series is the A4 paper size, which is 210 mm × 297 mm (8.27 in × 11.7 in)
