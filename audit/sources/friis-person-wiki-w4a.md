Harald Trap Friis (22 February 1893 – 15 June 1976), who published as H. T. Friis, was a Danish-American radio engineer whose work at Bell Laboratories included pioneering contributions to radio propagation, radio astronomy, and radar.
In 1946 Friis published his well-known analytic formula for power transfer between two antenna, the Friis transmission equation, which is still widely employed.
