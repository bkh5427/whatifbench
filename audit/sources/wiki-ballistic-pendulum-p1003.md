The ballistic pendulum was invented in 1742 by English mathematician Benjamin Robins (1707–1751)
published in his book New Principles of Gunnery, which revolutionized the science of ballistics
Robins' original work used a heavy iron pendulum, faced with wood, to catch the bullet.
Robins also used a length of ribbon, loosely gripped in a clamp, to measure the travel of the pendulum.
