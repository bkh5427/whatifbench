Title: "Folding Paper in Half—Twelve Times"
Author: Ivars Peterson
Publication Date: January 22, 2004
In January 2002, Gallivan herself demonstrated how to fold a large sheet of paper in half 12 times.
Gallivan worked out her own equations for the limit on the number times it's possible to fold a sheet of paper of a given size.
