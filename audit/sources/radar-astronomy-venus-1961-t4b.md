The first unambiguous detection of Venus was made by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory on 10 March 1961.
This led to early claims (from Lincoln Laboratory, Jodrell Bank, and Vladimir A. Kotelnikov of the USSR) which are now known to be incorrect.
Once the correct value was known, other groups found echos in their archived data that agreed with these results.
