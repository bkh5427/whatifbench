"The receiver minimum sensitivity test ensures a device under test (DUT) receives data with a defined maximum packet error rate (PER) of 10% at a defined minimum signal power."
"The minimum signal power depends on the channel bandwidth and modulation and coding scheme (MCS) as specified in Table 21-25 of IEEE Std 802.11™-2020"
