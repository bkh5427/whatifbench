Folding Paper in Half—Twelve Times
You can't fold a sheet of paper in half more than seven or eight times, no matter how large the sheet or thin the paper may be.
Several years ago when she was in high school, Britney Gallivan faced the challenge of folding a sheet in half an unheard-of 12 times.
After a lot of experimenting, Gallivan succeeded, folding a thin film of gold 12 times.
Gallivan worked out her own equations for the limit on the number times it's possible to fold a sheet of paper of a given size.
In January 2002, Gallivan herself demonstrated how to fold a large sheet of paper in half 12 times.
Gallivan described how she obtained her equations and her efforts to solve the folding problem in a booklet published by the Historical Society of Pomona Valley
